Use case: stylized-concept.
Create ONE inventive four-option visual concept board for IMITADOR (Mimic), an original chest-disguised creature for a rustic top-down survival-horror extraction game. The game uses ordinary wood loot chests, and the unopened mimic MUST be visually indistinguishable from one. No eyes, teeth, limbs, organic hints or glowing clues on CLOSED disguise. Show concepts for the REVEALED creature, with one shared closed chest inset to communicate the disguise.
The user values varied monster silhouettes and locomotion, not repeated four-legged humanoids. Each design must retain recognizable weathered dark-brown wood boards, simple dull iron straps and chest lid incorporated into an organic creature that can plausibly retract into that chest. Do NOT transform into a giant unrelated monster. Geometry practical to model/rig in Blender: hinged lid, simple wooden panels and sculpted organic volumes, manageable appendage count. Avoid huge tongues, many tentacles, complex simulation, tiny decorative clutter.
Landscape board, charcoal neutral background, four equal columns. Large complete three-quarter elevated REVEALED creature view above, smaller TRUE VERTICAL ORTHOGRAPHIC OVERHEAD view of EXACTLY THE SAME revealed creature below, front facing UP. Anatomy and chest opening direction must match between the two views. Generous margins, no cropping, restrained studio light, polished textured sculpted 3D concept-render aesthetic, grounded macabre horror, desaturated wood brown/charcoal/bone and dry muted flesh. No blood or exposed wet gore, no fantasy coins, magic, neon or technology. One small shared closed normal chest inset centered in the title area, not an extra concept column.
Title exactly "IMITADOR — OPÇÕES VISUAIS". Label shared closed inset exactly "Disfarce fechado". Option labels exactly:
"1 — MORDIDA DE TAMPA"
"2 — BAÚ BÍPEDE"
"3 — ESTÔMAGO RASTEJANTE"
"4 — DOBRADIÇA VIVA"

1 MORDIDA DE TAMPA: the chest itself becomes a wide jaw. Hinged rectangular wooden lid lifted at an angle, blunt bone teeth lining rim, black dry mouth cavity inside lower box. Exactly TWO muscular organic ARMS unfold from chest's sides and pull it along the ground, no legs. No long tongue. Sturdy hands in front, short organic body mostly contained by wood box. A readable lid-opening bite silhouette from overhead, mouth opening angled forward/up, no face elsewhere.
2 BAÚ BÍPEDE: exactly TWO short thick organic legs unfold beneath a compact wooden chest box, no arms. Upper wooden lid is slightly raised and forms a crooked jaw with teeth, soft mouth inside, box body kept chest-proportioned. Stands upright on stubby two-foot stance, uneven waddling gait. Biped chest silhouette distinctly different from first option, not a humanoid wearing a chest.
3 ESTÔMAGO RASTEJANTE: LEGLESS creature whose chest opens into a short thick soft slug-like belly, with a hinged wooden lid on the front upper jaw and lower wood panels framing the lower jaw. No arms, no legs and no external tentacles. Moves by compact waves of body compression along its soft exposed rear belly, short sturdy trailing stump, not a long snake. Front mouth opens upward-forward with a few thick teeth. Chest's whole wood surface can close again around retracted belly. Overhead shows rectangular jaw at front and tapered soft body behind.
4 DOBRADIÇA VIVA: a tall hinged OPEN chest whose lid and lower box are two wooden halves held apart by a thick curved organic spinal hinge along one edge, NOT mechanical hinges or actual springs. Exactly TWO long jointed organic LEGS under lower box, giving the box a tilted uneven upright stance; no arms or extra limbs. Spine short and thick enough to fold into closed box. Teeth are sparse at opposite edges of the two wooden halves, mouth is the dark gap between them. Strong angular silhouette and forward lid-clap bite, soft bone hinge visible from overhead. Must still read as a chest opening, no literal musical instrument, no accordion or humanoid costume.

Four distinct practical revealed creatures; closed disguise inset plain and identical for all. Never show eyes or teeth on unopened inset. No other text or watermark. Concept proposals, not actual existing Blender models.
