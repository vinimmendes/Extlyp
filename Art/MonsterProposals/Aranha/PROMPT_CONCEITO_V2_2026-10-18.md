## Especificação usada na geração

Use case: stylized-concept. One cohesive professional creature-design sheet for Cyber Extraction, an original top-down dark forest and abandoned catacomb extraction game. Use the existing approved pale spider and motion poses as the proportion and identity reference, not as the target rendering quality. Concept art, not a spritesheet or gameplay capture.

Landscape board, charcoal-olive background. Large three-quarter overhead hero at left; consistent true top-down view with all eight legs separated at right, a small side anatomy view, and three behavior sketches below: crouched dash anticipation, forward thrust, descending with tucked legs from one silk tether. Small asymmetrical dense ceiling-web study. Portuguese labels: ARANHA PÁLIDA, VISTA SUPERIOR, PERFIL, PREPARAÇÃO, DASH, DESCIDA. No paragraphs or pseudo-text.

Exactly eight articulated legs in four pairs. Pale ivory mottled pear-shaped abdomen, subtle longitudinal crest, irregular gray-olive staining, small dark warm-charcoal cephalothorax, eight tiny matte black eyes in one coherent cluster and two modest curved chelicerae. Preserve low wide footprint and pale identity. Replace joined spheres and straight conical sticks with tapered organic limbs, overlapping chitin sockets, rounded patella joints, dark flexible membranes and hooked tarsal tips. Expressive front legs, weight supported by rear pairs. Pale shafts transition to dark worn tips. Taut waxy chitin, a few broad ridges and shallow creases; sparse tactile bristles near feet and mouth. No fluffy fur, robot parts, armor plating, human skull, large bone spikes, glowing eyes, neon or gore.

Muted aged ivory, gray olive, brown-black. Restrained roughness variation; readable large forms before noisy microdetail. Warm gray-ivory web silk with uneven tension, broken strands and a denser center, not a perfect geometric wheel. Painterly game concept art with sculptable coherent anatomy, diffuse light revealing articulation, uncluttered neutral ground and small readability studies suitable for a 256 px sprite. No logos, HUD or ornate framing. Damp abandoned stone and forest horror atmosphere; original design rather than a copied franchise creature.

## Interpretação para produção

A vista superior e as regras do plano governam a futura implementação. As sugestões de cenário, movimento borrado e pequenas saliências da prancha não autorizam copiar fundo, motion blur, espinhos ou anatomia inconsistente para os sprites. Preservar oito patas, checagens de olhos, pivô, dimensões, câmera de exportação e tempos de gameplay no trabalho Blender.
