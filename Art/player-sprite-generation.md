Style/medium: gritty hand-painted 2D game sprite, coarse brush texture and restrained detail that remains readable at small gameplay scale, realistic adult proportions, not pixel art, not cartoon, not a polished 3D render.

Survivor facing EAST (right edge of canvas): crown of head toward the right, backpack toward the left, shoulder line roughly vertical on screen, feet foreshortened beneath torso. Constraints: genuinely transparent alpha background, isolated character only, no floor, no cast shadow outside silhouette, no environment, no text, no border, no labels, no watermark, no multiple views, no sprite sheet, no gun, no recognizable Darkwood protagonist costume.
