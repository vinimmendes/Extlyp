# Teste de caminhada com espingarda

Gerado em 2026-10-10 com image_gen integrado a partir da espingarda v4 aprovada. Folha de oito quadros em quatro colunas e duas linhas, leitura da esquerda para a direita, depois segunda linha. Quadros e GIF em `Assets/Art/Player/WalkTest/`.

Prévia: oito quadros a 8 fps, duração de um segundo, repetição contínua. O script `Art/assemble-walk-test.py` apenas separa as células geradas e empacota o GIF; nenhuma animação foi ligada ao player na Unity. Folha original 1774×887; quadros extraídos em 444×444 com até um pixel de preenchimento transparente.

Protótipo visual: geração pode variar detalhes, poses e alinhamento entre quadros. Não validado no jogo. O GIF usa paleta reduzida e transparência binária; os PNGs preservam RGBA.

## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a production animation SPRITE SHEET using the referenced approved top-down rustic survivor holding a double-barrel shotgun. Exactly EIGHT frames in an exact FOUR COLUMNS by TWO ROWS grid, every cell equal size, no gaps, no grid lines, no labels. Output landscape 2:1 aspect ratio. One complete character per cell with transparent margins, entire shotgun inside each cell. Read frames left to right first row then second row. Animation: one full in-place WALK CYCLE toward EAST/right, continuously aiming shotgun right, strict vertical overhead camera. Frame 1 left foot forward right foot back; frame 2 left contact weight settles; frame 3 feet passing; frame 4 right foot moving forward; frame 5 right foot forward left foot back; frame 6 right contact weight settles; frame 7 feet passing; frame 8 left foot moving forward back toward frame 1. Legs must visibly alternate fore/aft along horizontal east-west walking axis, never along vertical north-south axis. Natural small shoulder counter-motion and subtle backpack sway, torso bob of only a few pixels, arms remain steady holding gun. Walk not run. CRITICAL consistency: same person, same proportions, same character SCALE, same torso-center/pivot at the exact same normalized position in EVERY CELL, same weapon length, same clothes, patches, backpack, hairstyle and earthy palette. No rotation across frames, no size changes, no camera changes, no foot sliding through body, no detached limbs. Entire character stays facing east. Maintain approved hand grip and top view of shotgun. Old olive field jacket, brown trousers and boots, canvas backpack, gritty hand-painted detail consistent with reference, no technology. Genuinely transparent alpha background across sheet. No floor, no shadow outside silhouette, no text, no decorations.
