# Quebráveis — fontes e prompts

Seleção final: versões originais com leve inclinação, conforme preferência explícita do usuário. As tentativas posteriores de câmera totalmente vertical foram descartadas. A preferência final prevalece sobre as instruções ortográficas dos prompts abaixo. Os seis PNGs desta pasta e suas cópias de runtime são as versões selecionadas.

Ferramenta integrada image_gen. Atlas transparentes, duas células por variante: intacto à esquerda e destroços à direita. As urnas reutilizam os assets existentes CAT-P05 e CAT-P10; não foram geradas novamente.

## Crate v1

Create ONE production sprite atlas for Cyber Extraction, a grounded medieval catacomb survival game. Atlas variant Crate 1: square rotted oak crate with diagonal brace. TWO equal square cells in ONE horizontal 2:1 PNG, with genuine transparent alpha background. Left half: one intact object. Right half: same object smashed into a compact low pile of broken wooden boards, splinters and matching hoops or bindings. Both states strictly top-down orthographic camera straight vertically down, ZERO isometric perspective, no visible vertical side walls, same scale and same center in each half, each object/pile confined to inner 80 percent of its own cell, generous transparent gutters. Materials realistic hand-painted, aged damp wood and dull iron, muted brown/grey/olive, restrained broad upper-left lighting. Readable small game silhouette like a fragile container, no loot inside, no coins, no weapons. Original Darkwood-like abandonment with clear tactical silhouette. No floor, background, grid lines, border, labels, text, characters, detached shadows outside footprint, UI or glowing outlines. The right state is destroyed flat debris, not another intact container. Target 1536x768 pixels. Save PNG locally and return path.
