# Cabana abandonada — conceito v1

Gerado em 11/10/2026 com image_gen integrado. Imagem: `abandoned-cabin-concept-v1.png`. Proposta visual para revisão, sem integração no jogo e sem recortes de sprites.

## Direção solicitada

- Cabana abandonada reaproveitada como abrigo.
- Cama para dormir e recuperar vida; mecânica a implementar depois.
- Baú já indicado pelo usuário: preservar seu asset. O baú nesta pintura é conceitual.
- Fogão a lenha no lugar da fogueira, abastecido com madeira; consumo a definir depois.
- Lago exterior com acesso bloqueado por tronco e machado enferrujado preso. Terminar o corte concede madeira, quebra o machado e libera a pesca; substitui a ideia anterior de píer.
- Cerca e portão parcialmente quebrados, com caixa de correio velha junto à entrada.
- Bancada de munição começa quebrada e evolui para bancada improvisada, conforme orientação posterior do usuário. Primeiros sprites registrados em `sprites-primeiro-lote.md`.

A representação sem teto permite ver o piso central livre e os acessos a cama, baú, fogão e bancada. Caminhos exteriores ligam porta, píer e portão. Darkwood orienta atmosfera, abandono e materiais; Bullet Echo orienta legibilidade e circulação. Layout próprio, materiais rústicos, sem cyber e sem HUD.

É uma referência visual, sem métricas de gameplay validadas. Na próxima etapa, móveis, chão, paredes, água, margem, tronco, cerca, portão e caixa de correio poderão virar sprites/módulos separados. Estados de combustível e reparação serão definidos depois. O mockup atualizado sem HUD está em `cabin-gameplay-nohud-v2.png`, documentado em `gameplay-nohud-v2.md`; a imagem v1 abaixo permanece como histórico da proposta com píer.

Nenhum código, prefab ou cena foi alterado. Nenhum teste ou build foi executado.

## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: ONE original horizontal environment concept painting for a top-down 2D woodland survival extraction game's home base. Primary request: replace a camp with an abandoned wooden cabin reclaimed as a modest survivor refuge, with a healing/rest bed, existing-style storage chest, a wood-fueled cooking stove, an outdoor small pond and a repairable pier, a half-broken gate connected to a half-broken fence, and an old mailbox beside the gate. This is a VISUAL CONCEPT for future sprites and level work, not a finished tilemap or HUD.
Camera/composition: landscape 16:9 canvas, very high directly overhead game camera, near-orthographic clear top-down plan. Show the cabin WITHOUT ROOF as a clean cutaway so all interior furniture and walking space are clearly visible from above. Include the entire small property, the outdoor pond, gate and boundary fence in one coherent composition. Cabin occupies roughly the central-left half; a small finite pond is outside to the right, surrounded by visible shoreline, NOT a river or sea. Broken fence traces the lower and left perimeter of the property; its weathered crooked gate faces a narrow woodland dirt path leading out. One unmistakable old rusted rural mailbox on a leaning wooden post stands right next to this gate. Keep each interactive object separated and recognizable, with comfortable walking paths through cabin door to bed, chest, stove, outdoor pier and gate. Layout and spatial readability inspired by Bullet Echo; do not copy any existing layout or interface. Cabin's single open doorway faces the yard and gate. A path forks naturally from the doorway toward the lake and toward the gate.
Interior: aged plank floor, stained mossy timber walls, patched broken window openings. A narrow worn wooden BED with stained pillow and faded olive blanket in a sheltered corner, clearly a place to sleep and recover. A sturdy battered wood STORAGE CHEST with dark iron straps and lock near the bed but separated from it, recognizable top-down chest silhouette; it stands in for the game's existing chest asset rather than inventing a new storage system. One rusted enclosed cast-iron WOOD-BURNING COOKSTOVE against another wall with two top cooking plates, stovepipe/chimney and small stacked split FIREWOOD directly beside it to make wood fuel readable. Tiny restrained amber warmth around stove contrasts with the cold abandoned interior. NO open outdoor campfire; the cookstove is the cooking station. Retain a compact old crafting WORKBENCH along an interior wall, with restrained clutter and a clear approach. Large clear center of floor, no maze of rooms, no furniture blocking the entrance.
Exterior: shallow dark green woodland pond, reeds and muddy bank. A short small wooden PIER extends from shore into pond; part of decking is repaired with lighter timber while one outer side retains cracked planks and missing boards, visibly reconstructible, no large marina. Damaged aged wooden fence with leaning posts, missing pickets and fallen rails; gate is still identifiable and usable despite hanging crooked. Dense forest around perimeter, bare twisted branches, olive moss, sparse weeds, decaying leaves, damp earth. Leave the property itself readable, not hidden under foliage. Abandonment without excessive trash.
Style/medium: gritty hand-painted original 2D environmental concept art, realistic scale and consistent surfaces matching worn rustic survivor sprites. Atmospheric influence from Darkwood's oppressive woods, abandonment, lived-in ruined interiors and earthy detail, but no copied characters, assets, composition or logos. Muted olive, soot gray, wet dark wood, dirty beige and rust brown; soft overcast twilight, subtle local stove glow, slight cool mist only at outer forest edges. Enough ambient light to see all requested features. Uneasy outside, restrained sense of shelter inside. No futuristic technology or cyber design, no neon, no military barricades, no tents, no sci-fi portal. No people, no enemies, no UI, no labels, no text, no title, no numbered callouts, no watermark, no decorative concept-board border. Whole scene painted to edges, opaque background.
