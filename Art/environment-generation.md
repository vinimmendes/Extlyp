# Sprites do ambiente

Folha original gerada com a ferramenta integrada imagegen e salva em `Assets/Resources/Environment/AbandonedProps.png`. Seis sprites: arvore, arvore morta, armario de madeira, cama, mesa e armario de metal. O PNG original foi preservado; o Unity recorta os sprites por metadados e limites de alpha. Integrados apenas ao mapa grande.

## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE transparent environment sprite atlas for an original top-down 2D survival horror game. Exactly SIX separate sprites in a perfectly regular 3-column by 2-row equal-cell grid, generous transparent gutters, no overlap, each object centered in its cell. Top row left to right: (1) a dense gnarled forest tree crown seen from straight above, irregular olive and moss canopy with weathered dark branches, (2) a dead tree seen from straight above, a thick central stump and twisted leafless radial branches, (3) an old abandoned wooden wardrobe seen from straight above, battered brown timber top with cracked boards and subtle rusted fittings. Bottom row left to right: (4) an abandoned narrow bed seen from straight above, stained beige mattress, faded dark green torn blanket and dirty pillow, weathered wood frame, (5) an old rectangular wooden table seen from straight above, scarred plank surface, one tarnished plate and one rag, (6) a small rusted steel storage cabinet seen from straight above, chipped olive-gray painted metal and brown rust patches. Camera STRICTLY overhead 90 degrees, orthographic, flat ground plane; do not show front-facing wardrobe doors or cabinet doors, do not render isometric or perspective. Medium: high-quality hand-painted 2D game sprites, gritty desaturated woodland horror mood, earthy brown, muted olive, tarnished gray, distressed surfaces and restrained contrast, clear recognizable silhouettes, suitable for dim lighting. Only the six isolated props; no floors, rooms, houses, scenes, pedestal, UI borders, text, labels, logos, people, or watermark. True transparent alpha background including gutters and between branches. No broad external cast shadows. Equal scale of framing rather than real-world sizes, each sprite occupies about 75 percent of its own cell and remains entirely contained. Landscape canvas 3:2 aspect ratio.
